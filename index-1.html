What an IP address can tell you

An IP address may indicate a network or internet provider and a broad approximate region. It usually cannot identify a person's exact home address or prove who sent a message.

Addresses can be shared, dynamic, masked by VPNs, or belong to mobile carriers, businesses, and public networks.

ScamWatch does not collect visitors' IP addresses, create tracking links, run port scans, or attempt to access devices. Use platform reporting tools and preserve messages instead.
